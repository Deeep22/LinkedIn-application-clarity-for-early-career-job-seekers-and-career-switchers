# LinkedIn: Application Clarity for Early-Career Job Seekers

A product management case study on the gap between applying for a job on LinkedIn and learning what happened to the application.

**Segment:** early-career candidates and career switchers
**Focus:** two journey stages, evaluating fit before applying and waiting after applying

**What's inside**
- Problem framing and current-state analysis (LinkedIn's employer-synced application statuses and where they leave gaps)
- First-hand observation: a log of 20 of my own applications, with counts only
- Success metrics, with guardrails for the recruiter side
- Six solution options scored with RICE, and a recommended MVP (freshness signal plus status with fallback states)
- Figma wireframes, risks and tradeoffs, and next steps

**Evidence note:** this is outside-in analysis plus my own application log. Pain points beyond that are labeled as hypotheses, and no user interviews are included yet.
